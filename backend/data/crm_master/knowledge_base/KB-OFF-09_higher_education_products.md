# Higher Education Products: Pearson+, MyLab, Mastering, Revel

Excerpts are short quotations from the cited page, collected on 2026-11-04. The summaries are our own words. These are marketing descriptions, not feature specifications.

## Pearson+
> "Pearson+ empowers students to choose how they learn best with easy-to-use eTextbooks and study tools." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/products-services.html))

## MyLab
> "MyLab gives you the tools to easily customize your course and guide students to real results." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/products-services.html))

## Mastering
> "Mastering supports the way science and engineering students learn best: through engaging, immersive experiences." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/products-services.html))

## Revel
> "Revel enables students to explore and apply concepts as they read, encouraging active participation in learning." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/products-services.html))

## Matching customer needs (illustrative guidance)
- Gateway or high-enrolment courses that need practice and course customisation: start with MyLab.
- Science and engineering departments: start with Mastering.
- A reading-led, active-learning course: look at Revel.
- Students who want an eTextbook and study tools: Pearson+.
- A product description is not proof that a specific requirement is met. Use the product fit checklist (KB-PRD-02) and confirm gaps with a solutions consultant.
