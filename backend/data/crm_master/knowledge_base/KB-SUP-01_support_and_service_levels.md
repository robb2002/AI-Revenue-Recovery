# Support and Service Levels Overview

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It describes support in general terms. It deliberately contains no response-time or uptime figures, because none are approved for this demo.

## Purpose
Customers often ask what happens when something goes wrong during term. This overview says what can be said without a contractual commitment.

## Support routes
- Instructors and students use the product's help pages and the support contact shown in the product.
- Institution administrators have a named account contact for escalations.
- Integration problems with an LMS go through the LMS administrator first, then to Integration Services (KB-INT-01).

## Severity thinking
| Level | Example | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Most users cannot sign in during an exam or assessment window | Immediate escalation to the account contact and support lead |
| High | A core feature is unavailable for a course | Same-day review by support |
| Normal | A single user issue or question | Standard queue |

The targets for each level are set in the customer's agreement and are not listed here.

## What a representative may say
- That support exists for each product and that an escalation route is named in the agreement.
- That peak periods, such as the first weeks of term, are planned for with Customer Success.

## What a representative must not say
- A guaranteed response or resolution time.
- A percentage uptime figure.
- That support is available at all hours, unless Customer Support confirms it in writing.

## Not stated in this document
- Response and resolution targets.
- Uptime commitments and service credits.
- Support hours by region.

Questions on these go to Customer Support with the product and the customer's country.
