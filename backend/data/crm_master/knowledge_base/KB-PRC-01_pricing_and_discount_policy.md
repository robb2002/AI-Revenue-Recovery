# Pricing and Discount Policy

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It describes a plausible internal process for the demo. It contains no real prices and no real approval limits.

## Purpose
This policy tells a sales representative what can be said about price before a written quote exists, and who must approve a discount. It exists so that a customer never receives a figure that has not been approved.

## What a representative may say
- Price depends on the product, the number of learners, the term, and the services included.
- A written quote from the quoting tool is the only valid price statement.
- Published list prices are not stored in this knowledge base. Do not quote a figure from memory or from an old email.

## What a representative must not say
- A final price, a discount percentage, or a "best price" before approval.
- That a competitor's price will be beaten.
- That a discount is available "for this term only" unless Revenue Operations has confirmed it in writing.

## Discount approval
1. The representative submits a discount request with the account, products, learner count, term, and the business reason.
2. The sales manager reviews first. Larger requests go to Revenue Operations for written approval. The thresholds are set by Finance and are not listed here (NEEDS VERIFICATION in a real deployment).
3. Approval is recorded against the opportunity before the quote is sent.
4. Any change to scope after approval needs a new request.

## Multi-year and bundled offers
Multi-year terms and bundles may change the structure of a quote. The structure must come from the quoting tool and be approved. A representative should not combine products into a bundle verbally.

## Public-sector and tender pricing
Tenders usually require a fixed price schedule and a validity period. Send the tender to Revenue Operations early. Do not submit a price schedule without approval.

## How the assistant should use this document
- If asked for a final price, discount level, or approval limit, the answer is that this document does not contain it. Offer the approval steps and route the question to Revenue Operations.
- Never produce a number that is not in an approved quote.
