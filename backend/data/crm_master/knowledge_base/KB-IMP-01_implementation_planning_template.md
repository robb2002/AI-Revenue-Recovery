# Implementation Planning Template

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It sets out the phases of a typical rollout. It contains no delivery commitments. Dates and durations are agreed with Customer Success for each project.

## Purpose
Customers worry that the product will not be ready for term start. This template shows the order of work, so the representative can set a realistic expectation.

## Phases
### 1. Kick-off
Agree goals, the named contacts on both sides, products in scope, and the target start date.

### 2. Technical set-up
Connect the LMS (see KB-INT-01), set up sign-on, and create test courses and users.

### 3. Content and course set-up
Select titles, build course shells, and agree grading rules. Note the grade sync limits in KB-OFF-02.

### 4. Instructor readiness
Run briefings and give instructors a short guide. Schedule extra sessions for new users.

### 5. Pilot or soft launch
A small group of courses use the product first. Collect issues and decide whether to widen.

### 6. Go-live and review
Launch to all courses in scope. Hold a review after the first weeks of term and record the outcome.

## Planning rules
- Work backward from the customer's term start date.
- A pilot-first plan reduces risk when the date is tight.
- Name an owner for every phase.
- Do not promise a number of weeks. Ask Customer Success for the plan.

## Common causes of delay
- LMS administrator not available.
- Titles chosen late.
- Procurement or contract still open when set-up is meant to begin.
- Instructors not briefed before term.

## What to record on the opportunity
Target start date, phase owners, open risks, and the date of the next check-in.

## Escalation
If the requested go-live date falls inside the first set-up phase, ask Customer Success to review before replying.
