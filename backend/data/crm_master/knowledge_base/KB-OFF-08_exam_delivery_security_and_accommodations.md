# Exam Delivery, Identity Checks and Accommodations (Pearson VUE)

Excerpts are short quotations from the cited pages, collected on 2026-11-04. The summaries are our own words. This document applies to Pearson VUE exam delivery, not to courseware.

## Confirming who is taking the exam
Summary (our words): the test-owner page lists identity measures used at check-in.

> "Enforce stringent identity confirmation protocols, including digital photo, signature capture, and palm-vein scanning" ([source](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-owners/secure.html))

## Protecting exam content and monitoring
> "Encrypt and audit assets with proprietary exam delivery software" ([source](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-owners/secure.html))

> "Audit online test-taker behavior in real time with live proctors and AI" ([source](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-owners/secure.html))

## Accommodations
Summary (our words): accommodations are decided case by case, together with the exam programme, and the process varies by programme.

> "Test accommodations are individualized and considered on a case-by-case basis by or in partnership with your testing program." ([source](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations.html))

> "Each exam program has specific processes and requirements." ([source](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/test-takers/accommodations.html))

## Assistive technology
> "Understanding that candidates prefer to use assistive technologies with which they are familiar, Pearson VUE software supports a variety of open-source and commercially available assistive technologies." ([source](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/legal/accessibility-statement/policy.html))

## Illustrative guidance for certification and government customers
- The customer's exam programme sets the identity and accommodation rules, so confirm them with the programme owner.
- Do not promise specific test-centre availability in a given city. That is not on these pages.

## Not covered by this document
- Test-centre locations and capacity in a named country.
- Data retention for biometric identity records.
- Pricing for exam delivery.
