# Data Transfers and Hosting Locations

Excerpts are short quotations from the cited pages, collected on 2026-11-04. The summaries are our own words.

## There is no single global hosting statement
Summary (our words): the cited pages describe hosting and transfers in general terms, and one product notice gives a specific location. Hosting location depends on the product. Do not give one answer for every product.

## General statement on transfers
> "Many of Pearson's products and services are developed, delivered and hosted entirely in the EEA and/or UK" ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/privacy-center/international-data-transfers.html))

> "This may include transfers of personal information to the United States of America." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/privacy-center/international-data-transfers.html))

## Safeguards named for transfers
> "Pearson relies on EU Commission adequacy decisions and Standard Contractual Clauses" ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/privacy-center/international-data-transfers.html))

## Retention
> "Customer personal data is retained only for the period necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in the respective privacy notices unless a longer retention period is required or allowed by applicable law(s)." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/privacy-center/international-data-transfers.html))

## Example of a product-specific location
Summary (our words): this is the notice for one product only. It must not be generalised to other products.

> "The Pearson Communication Coach processes and stores data on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure located in the Republic of Ireland and Sweden" ([source](https://www.pearson.com/privacy-center/privacy-notices/WLA-privacy-notice.html))

> "All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using tenant-specific encryption keys" ([source](https://www.pearson.com/privacy-center/privacy-notices/WLA-privacy-notice.html))

## Not covered by this document
- Hosting locations for MyLab, Mastering, Revel, Pearson+, Pearson VUE, PTE, or Versant.
- Whether a customer can choose a hosting region, or have data held in a specific country such as India or Australia.
- A statement that data is hosted only in the United States.
- Contract-level data residency terms. Refer these to Legal (KB-LGL-01).
