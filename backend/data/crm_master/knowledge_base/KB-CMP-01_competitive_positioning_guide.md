# Competitive Positioning Guide (Category Level)

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It teaches a way of handling competitive questions. It names no competitor product, makes no feature comparison, and quotes no competitor price.

## Purpose
When a customer says "we are also looking at another provider", the representative needs a calm, accurate response that does not disparage or invent.

## Principles
- Ask what the customer values before talking about Pearson.
- Describe only Pearson capabilities that appear in approved documents (KB-OFF-09, KB-OFF-10, KB-PRD-02).
- Do not state facts about a competitor. If the customer shares a claim, record it as the customer's claim.
- Never promise that a price will be lower or that a feature is better.

## Questions to ask
- What is the main reason you are comparing?
- Which requirements are non-negotiable?
- Who else is involved in the evaluation, and when do they decide?
- What would make you comfortable choosing one option?

## Responding to common situations
| Situation | Approach |
|---|---|
| The customer has used another platform for years | Ask what works well and what does not. Offer a pilot to compare in context |
| The customer says a competitor is cheaper | Do not argue price. Refer to the pricing policy (KB-PRC-01) and offer an approved quote |
| The customer says a competitor has a feature | Record the claim. Use the product fit checklist (KB-PRD-02) |
| The customer is bundling a purchase with an LMS provider | Focus on integration facts (KB-OFF-01, KB-OFF-02) |

## What to record
The competitor name as said by the customer, the reason, the decision date, and who made the claim.

## Escalation
For a formal competitive evaluation or tender, ask Product Marketing for approved comparison material before replying.
