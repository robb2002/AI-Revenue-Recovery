# Student Data and Privacy Notices

Excerpts are short quotations from the cited pages, collected on 2026-11-04. The summaries are our own words.

## What student data can be involved
Summary (our words): when a learner uses an educational course, the notice lists the kinds of learner work and results that can be processed.

> "This personal data may include assignments, student coursework, responses to interactive exercises, assessments, scores, grades, and teacher/instructor comments." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/privacy-center/privacy-notices/full-privacy-notice.html))

## Institution-collected data
Summary (our words): data an institution collects itself is governed by that institution's own privacy policy.

> "Personal data collected by an educational institution or education provider in connection with their courses and programs are subject to the privacy policies of such educational institution or education provider." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/privacy-center/privacy-notices/full-privacy-notice.html))

## Pearson's role for one K-12 assessment reporting site
Summary (our words): for that reporting site, Pearson describes itself as a processor acting for the education agency. This is one site, not a statement about every product.

> "Pearson serves as a data Processor, and Pearson collects, processes, and uses Personal Data through this Site" ([source](https://familyportal.pearson.com/PrivacyPolicy.html))

## How to use this in a sales conversation (illustrative guidance)
- Quote the notices and link them. Do not summarise legal obligations in your own words.
- Questions about a signed data-processing agreement, deletion timelines, or student-privacy laws for a specific contract go to Legal (KB-LGL-01).

## Not covered by this document
- Contract-specific terms for any customer.
- Deletion or return of data at contract end.
- A claim of compliance with any named student-privacy law for a named product.
- Rules for children under a particular age in a particular country.
