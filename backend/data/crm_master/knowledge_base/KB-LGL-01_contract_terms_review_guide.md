# Contract Terms Review Guide

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It is not legal advice and it contains no real contract terms. It shows how a representative should handle contract questions.

## Purpose
Contract questions can stall a deal for weeks. This guide says what a representative may do and when to bring in Legal.

## What a representative may do
- Share standard documents that Legal has approved for sharing.
- Explain the review process and who owns each step.
- Note the customer's requested changes without agreeing to them.

## What a representative must not do
- Agree to changes in liability, indemnity, data protection, or governing law.
- State that a clause "is standard" or "will be accepted".
- Edit a contract document.

## Common requests and handling
| Request | Handling |
|---|---|
| Customer's own contract template | Send to Legal with the decision date |
| Data-processing agreement | Legal prepares it. Public notices are in KB-OFF-05 and KB-OFF-06 |
| Data hosting in a chosen country | Not covered in public pages. Legal and Security confirm |
| Payment terms | Finance and Revenue Operations |
| Auto-renewal and termination | Legal |
| Accessibility obligations | Product Accessibility with Legal (KB-OFF-07) |

## Review steps
1. Log the customer's draft and requested changes against the opportunity.
2. Send to Legal with the target signature date.
3. Legal returns a mark-up or a list of questions.
4. The representative shares only approved text.
5. Track each round with a date, so the delay is visible.

## Signals that legal review may slow the deal
- A new customer legal contact appears late in the process.
- The customer's draft is long and has not been shared before the quote.
- The signature date is fixed by the customer's term start.

## Escalation
Send any custom clause request to Legal the same day.
