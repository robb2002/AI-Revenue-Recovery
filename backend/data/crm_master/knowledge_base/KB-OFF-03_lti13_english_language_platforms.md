# LTI 1.3 Integration for English Language Learning Platforms

Excerpts are short quotations from the cited page, collected on 2026-11-04. The summaries are our own words.

## Standard used
> "Pearson's LMS integration for English Language Learning platforms is built on the LTI 1.3 standard." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/languages/en-us/why-pearson/seamless-lms-integrations.html))

## Supported platforms for this product line
Summary (our words): this page lists four platforms. The higher-education courseware page (KB-OFF-01) lists six. The lists differ by product line, so always ask which product the customer means.

> "Pearson's LTI 1.3 integration is supported on Blackboard, Canvas, Brightspace (D2L), and Moodle." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/languages/en-us/why-pearson/seamless-lms-integrations.html))

> "Integration is only available for these four LMS platforms." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/languages/en-us/why-pearson/seamless-lms-integrations.html))

## Customers on another LMS
> "If your institution uses a different LMS, please contact your Pearson representative to discuss available options." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/languages/en-us/why-pearson/seamless-lms-integrations.html))

## Who sets it up
Summary (our words): the customer's LMS administrator or IT team leads setup.

> "The integration setup is led by your institution's LMS Admin or IT team." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/languages/en-us/why-pearson/seamless-lms-integrations.html))

## Not covered by this document
- Timelines for setup.
- Support for platforms outside the four listed.
- Whether a specific customer's LMS configuration meets the requirements.
