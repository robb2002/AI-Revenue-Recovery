# Procurement Documentation Checklist

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It lists the documents customers commonly request during procurement and who provides each one. Availability of any item must be confirmed internally.

## Purpose
Reduce delay when a purchase moves into procurement, tender, or vendor registration.

## Questions to ask early
- Is a tender, quotation, or direct purchase required?
- Is there a framework or approved vendor list we need to be on?
- What is the earliest and latest award date?
- Who is the procurement contact, and is the buyer the same as the academic sponsor?

## Common documents and owners
| Document | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor registration form | Sales Operations | Needs company details; allow review time on the customer side |
| Quotation | Revenue Operations | Must follow the pricing policy (KB-PRC-01) |
| Security questionnaire | Information Security | See KB-SEC-01 |
| Accessibility documents | Product Accessibility | The public page names a request address (KB-OFF-07) |
| Data-processing terms | Legal | See KB-LGL-01 |
| Insurance and tax forms | Finance | Confirm which are required in the customer's country |
| Reference customers | Customer Success | Only with the reference customer's consent |

## Tender response tips
- Calendar the tender questions deadline and the submission deadline on the opportunity.
- Answer only what is asked. Quote approved text with its source.
- Mark any unanswered question and escalate it to the owner above.

## Typical delays
- Documents requested after the quote has been sent.
- Missing signatory or purchase order number.
- The customer's legal team receiving terms late.

## Escalation
Raise any tender with a fixed deadline to Sales Operations on the day it is received.
