# Grade Sync Between MyLab and an LMS Gradebook

Excerpts are short quotations from the cited help page, collected on 2026-11-04. The summaries are our own words.

## Direction of the sync
Summary (our words): grades move from MyLab to the LMS, in one direction.

> "Syncing is one-way, from your MyLab gradebook to your LMS gradebook." ([source](https://help.pearsoncmg.com/mfdl/instructor/Content/work_in_your_lms.htm))

## Timing
Summary (our words): grades sync as students finish work, with a possible short delay, and quiz or test scores wait until the student submits.

> "Grades automatically sync when students complete their assignments, but there might be a brief delay." ([source](https://help.pearsoncmg.com/mfdl/instructor/Content/work_in_your_lms.htm))

> "Scores on a test or quiz are not synced until students select Submit for the assignment." ([source](https://help.pearsoncmg.com/mfdl/instructor/Content/work_in_your_lms.htm))

## Known limits
Summary (our words): some MyLab gradebook features do not carry over, and edits should be made in the MyLab gradebook.

> "Some MyLab features cannot be synced with your LMS gradebook:" ([source](https://help.pearsoncmg.com/mfdl/instructor/Content/work_in_your_lms.htm))

> "Dropped lowest scores" ([source](https://help.pearsoncmg.com/mfdl/instructor/Content/work_in_your_lms.htm))

> "Change assignment names, chapter associations, and students' grades only in your MyLab gradebook." ([source](https://help.pearsoncmg.com/mfdl/instructor/Content/work_in_your_lms.htm))

## What this means for a sales conversation (illustrative guidance)
- If a customer asks whether grades can be sent back to the LMS, the documented answer is yes, from MyLab to the LMS.
- If a customer expects grades to flow from the LMS into MyLab, that is not described here. Do not promise it.
- Customers who rely on dropped-lowest-score rules should be told the limit above and referred to a solutions consultant.

## Not covered by this document
- Whether the same behaviour applies to products other than MyLab.
- How long "a brief delay" is.
- Behaviour for a customised or older LMS version.
