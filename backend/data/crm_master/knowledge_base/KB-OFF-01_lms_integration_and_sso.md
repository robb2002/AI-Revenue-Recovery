# LMS Integration and Sign-On for Higher Education Courseware

Excerpts are short quotations from the cited page, collected on 2026-11-04. Pages change, so re-check before relying on them. The summaries are our own words, not Pearson's wording.

## Which learning management systems are listed
Summary (our words): the page for higher-education courseware lists six learning management systems, including Brightspace by D2L.

> "Pearson LMS integration works with Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace by D2L, Moodle, Schoology and Sakai." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/educators/digital-learning-platforms/lms-integration-services.html))

## What the integration provides
Summary (our words): sign-on through the LMS, grade sync, and assignment linking are described, along with an LTI certification statement.

> "With single sign-on through your Learning Management System into Pearson, students are ready on their first day." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/educators/digital-learning-platforms/lms-integration-services.html))

> "Sync grades with ease from MyLab and Mastering products into the LMS gradebook." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/educators/digital-learning-platforms/lms-integration-services.html))

> "Link to MyLab & Mastering products and content areas from within your LMS." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/educators/digital-learning-platforms/lms-integration-services.html))

> "Pearson meets industry standards and is recognized by IMS Global as LTI Certified." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/educators/digital-learning-platforms/lms-integration-services.html))

## What institutional data is stored
Summary (our words): the page says institutional data is not stored beyond anonymised identifiers used for grade sync.

> "Pearson uses LMS Standards and does NOT store any institutional data with the exception of anonymized user IDs and Course IDs for the purposes of grade sync." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/educators/digital-learning-platforms/lms-integration-services.html))

## How an institution starts
Summary (our words): availability for a specific institution's LMS is confirmed through a Pearson sales representative.

> "Contact your Pearson sales representative to learn about Access Pearson availability for your school's LMS and to request setup." ([source](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/higher-education/educators/digital-learning-platforms/lms-integration-services.html))

## Not covered by this document
These points are not stated on the cited page. Treat them as unconfirmed and ask the integration or product team.
- Direct sign-on with SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect without an LMS. The page describes sign-on through the LMS only.
- Support for any LMS not named above, or for a specific LMS version.
- Whether a particular institution is eligible for the integration.
- Two-way grade exchange. See KB-OFF-02 for what is said about grade direction.
