# LMS Integration Planning Guide

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It is a planning aid for sales and customer teams. What the integration supports is stated in the official excerpts KB-OFF-01, KB-OFF-02, and KB-OFF-03. This guide does not restate or extend them.

## Purpose
Help a representative gather what is needed to confirm an LMS integration, so the answer to a customer is accurate and the project does not stall.

## Information to collect from the customer
| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| LMS name and version | The supported platforms differ by product line (see KB-OFF-01 and KB-OFF-03) |
| Hosting model of the LMS (cloud or self-hosted) | Affects who performs setup |
| LMS administrator contact | Setup is led by the customer's LMS administrator or IT team (KB-OFF-03) |
| Products in scope | MyLab, Mastering, Revel, and language platforms are not identical |
| Sign-on approach | Through the LMS, or another identity system |
| Gradebook expectations | Grade direction is stated in KB-OFF-02 |
| Courses and term start date | Drives the order of work |

## Planning sequence
1. Confirm product and LMS pairing against the official excerpts.
2. Where the pairing is not listed, raise it with Integration Services and tell the customer it is being confirmed.
3. Agree a test course and test users before the term starts.
4. Walk through grade sync with an instructor using a sample assignment.
5. Agree a named contact for each side during the first weeks of term.

## Common concerns and where to look
- "Can grades go both ways?" See KB-OFF-02.
- "What data does the integration keep?" See KB-OFF-01.
- "Can we use our own sign-on system?" Not covered in the public excerpts. Escalate.

## Risks to note on the opportunity
- The customer's IT team has not been introduced.
- The LMS version is unknown.
- Go-live is planned for the first week of term with no test period.

## Escalation
Send unlisted LMS platforms and custom sign-on requests to Integration Services with the LMS name, version, and decision date.
