# Business Case Template

Illustrative document, SYNTHETIC. It is a template to help a customer sponsor request budget. It contains no real outcome data.

## Purpose
A sponsor often has to justify spend to a committee. This template helps them do it with facts the customer owns.

## Structure
### 1. The problem
State the issue in one paragraph, using the customer's own words and data. Example: low completion in a gateway course, or exam centres that cannot keep up with demand.

### 2. Who is affected
List learners, instructors, and departments, with the numbers the customer provides.

### 3. Options considered
Include doing nothing, a pilot, and a full rollout. A pilot option often helps when budget is uncertain.

### 4. Costs
Insert the approved quote from the quoting tool. Do not estimate. If there is no quote yet, write "quote pending" and name the date.

### 5. Expected benefits
Only use measurements the customer has agreed to track. Do not insert claims about improvement that no approved case study supports (NEEDS VERIFICATION for any outside figure).

### 6. Risks and dependencies
Examples: sign-on set-up, instructor training time, procurement steps.

### 7. Timeline and decision date
Show the date the committee meets, the date the quote expires, and the term start date.

## Budget-cycle questions to ask
- When does the budget year start and end?
- Is spend approved by one person or a committee?
- Is there an existing budget line that can be reused?
- Does a pilot need separate approval?

## Escalation
If the customer asks for return-on-investment figures, ask Sales Enablement which approved case material exists. Do not write numbers into the case.
